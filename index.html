Put it on your home screen

  1. Tap ▶ OPEN PIPEPRO above (must be in Safari)
  2. Tap the Share button ⮭ at the bottom of Safari
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add
  4. Launch from the new icon — full screen, works offline in the field
Heads up: do the Add to Home Screen from the app page itself (after tapping OPEN PIPEPRO), not from this page — that way the icon opens straight into the app.

What it does

⌗
ISO spool drawings — draw pipe runs with a compass pad (N/S/E/W/UP/DN, 45° offsets), tap fittings to edit. Valves, olets, reducers, socket weld or butt weld.
📄
PDF fab sheets — one tap makes a bordered 2-sheet package: iso drawing + BOM, cut list, and shop/field weld map. AirDrop or text it to the shop.
✂
Cut calculator — takeouts per ASME B16.9/B16.11 for any size, material, and end fitting. Center-to-center in, cut length out.
⊞
Tape calculator — feet-inches-sixteenths math with running totals and history. No more napkin arithmetic.
◯
Tools — flange bolt-up (count, stud size, gasket, material per B16.5), pipe circumference & volume, process assembly checklists, unit converter.
