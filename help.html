Install on iPhone

  1. Open Safari on the iPhone and go to the PipePro address (ask for the current link).
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow) in the Safari toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Launch PipePro from the home screen icon — it runs full-screen like a real app.
Good to know
Drawings and settings are saved on the phone — closing the app never loses work. The app remembers your default pipe size, material, and schedule (set them in MORE ▸ ⚙ SETTINGS).

Drawing ISOs DRAW

Jobs & drawings

Work is organized as Jobs (plant + area) that hold ISO drawings (one per line you're fabbing). Tap + NEW JOB, name it, then + ISO to start a drawing. Tap any drawing in the list to reopen it; ✕ deletes.

Drawing with the compass pad

  1. Tap the length chip (bottom-left) and enter the run's center-to-center length — feet, inches, and 16ths, or tap 🎤 and say it ("five feet six and a half").
  2. Tap a direction on the compass: N / S / E / W for plan directions, UP / DN for vertical.
  3. Tap GO — the run is drawn and the glowing end moves to the new point.
45° offsets
Tap two perpendicular directions (like N then E, or E then UP) before GO — PipePro draws the 45° rolled run and drops 45° ells at the corners automatically.

Fittings figure themselves out

Corners become 90° LR ells (tap the fitting to switch to short-radius), gentle corners become 45° ells, and a third leg into a fitting makes it a TEE. Weld numbers (W1, W2…) appear at every joint.

PDF Fab Sheets PDF SHEET

Tap PDF SHEET in the editor. You get a live preview of both sheets, then ⇪ SHARE / SAVE PDF builds a letter-landscape, 2-sheet fabrication package:

Sheet 1Bordered iso drawing with dimensions, cut lengths, flow arrows, weld tags, north arrow, and title block
Sheet 2Bill of material, cut list (C-C − takeouts = cut length), and shop/field weld map
On iPhone
The share sheet pops up — AirDrop it, text it, or Save to Files. If the share sheet doesn't appear, tap the ⇪ OPEN PDF button that appears under the export button.

Takeouts follow ASME B16.9 (LR ells, 45s, tees) and cut lengths deduct valve face-to-face dimensions you entered.

Cut Calculator CUT

  1. Pick pipe size and material (grouped by alloy family — carbon, chrome-moly, stainless, duplex, nickel, non-ferrous).
  2. Pick the connection type: butt weld, socket weld (≤ 4"), or threaded.
  3. Choose the fitting on each end — the takeout for each shows instantly.
  4. Enter the center-to-center distance. The big green number is your cut length.
Valve ends
If an end is a valve, tap ⚙ VALVE CONFIG to spec it: weld-in vs bolt-up, ANSI class (#150–#2500), face (RF/FF/RTJ), and flange end type. Bolt-up mode auto-generates the per-joint material list — flanges, gasket spec, stud count & diameter, and the right bolt metallurgy for the pipe material.

Tape Calculator TAPE

A construction calculator that thinks in feet-inches-sixteenths. Type feet and inches on the keypad (tap the FT or IN slot first), pick a fraction chip, and use + − =. Every result also reads out in decimal inches, millimeters, and decimal feet.

Field Tips

Workflow that works
Measure the line → draw it run-by-run with the compass → tag which welds are field welds → hit PDF SHEET and AirDrop the package to the shop. The BOM, cut list, and weld map build themselves from the drawing.
Always verify
Takeouts are standard ASME dimensions. Verify field dimensions before cutting, and confirm flange class and gasket specs against the job's piping class and P&ID.
